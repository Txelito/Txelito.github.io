About Me

Abstract

I'm Michel Silva Gonçalves, a Master's student in Digital Humanities at the University of Minho, with a first degree in Applied Languages. My work sits where language, data and artificial intelligence meet: natural language processing, data analysis, and how people and organisations actually adopt AI.

My internship report studies the implementation of AI in a business consultancy along three axes: cybersecurity, automation and AI agents, and strategic market analysis.

I'm also interested in the sociolinguistics of languages in contact, in particular Portuguese and Creole in Cape Verde and Guinea-Bissau.

I'm Michel Silva Gonçalves, working at the intersection of language, data and artificial intelligence.

I build practical tools that help organisations structure information, automate processes and make better-informed decisions: LLM-based assistants, automation pipelines, and cloud infrastructure.

I joined Progest as a Master's curricular intern and stayed on with a permanent contract, in the Digital Transformation department. My background in Applied Languages and Digital Humanities means I don't just build systems, I can also explain them to people who are not technical.
