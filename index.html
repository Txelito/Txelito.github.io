About Me

My work focuses on connecting technology, data and communication. I’m particularly interested in building practical digital solutions, analysing information and translating complex systems into insights that people and organisations can actually use.

With a background in Applied Languages and Digital Humanities, I combine technical curiosity with a strong understanding of language, text and communication. This allows me to approach technological problems not only from a technical perspective, but also from the perspective of how people interact with information.
